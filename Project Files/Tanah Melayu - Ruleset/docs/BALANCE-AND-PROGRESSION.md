# Progression and balance specification

## Standard progression
The tech tree runs Agriculture → Pottery/Animal Husbandry → Archery/Mining/Sailing → Bronze Working/Writing/Trapping → Iron Working/Mathematics/Calendar → Construction/Currency/Optics → Engineering/Education/Steel → Machinery/Compass/Guilds → Navigation/Printing Press/Gunpowder → Astronomy/Banking/Metallurgy → Economics/Chemistry/Steam Power → Industrialization.

Buildings require their listed technologies and production costs. Unique units do not grant instant technologies.

## Melaka MAX
Melaka receives large civilization-level yield and combat bonuses, enhanced naval mobility, and capital bonuses; it has an exclusive Royal Treasury and Laksamana Laut. It is intentionally far stronger than peers, but still uses the same research and production systems.

## Tuning warning
Unciv's unique strings are parsed by exact patterns. The included local validator checks JSON, IDs, prerequisites, and references, but the current client may reject unsupported unique patterns or fields. In-game validation remains required.
